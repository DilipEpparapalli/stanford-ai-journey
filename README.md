# Stanford AI Journey 🧠

> *Learning how AI works, from scratch, in public.*

---

## Why I'm doing this

We live in a world being shaped by AI  every day, in ways most people don't fully understand.

I want to change that for myself.

I want to know **how AI actually works** — not just how to use it, but what's happening underneath. How does a machine make a decision? How does it learn? How do you build something intelligent? How do you use it well, and think about it clearly?

To answer those questions, I needed a place to start. And in a world full of information, I found one of the best: **Stanford University's free AI lectures.**

So that's what I'm doing. Working through Stanford's AI curriculum — from the very beginning — and documenting everything I understand along the way.

---

## How I'm learning

I watch each Stanford lecture, then I work through every concept with **Claude (by Anthropic)** as my tutor — one idea at a time.

The process for every lecture:

1. **Watch** the Stanford lecture
2. **Discuss and understand** each concept deeply with Claude — back and forth, question by question, until it genuinely clicks
3. **Teach it back** in my own words — no moving forward until I can explain it clearly
4. **Test myself** with problems, from simple to hard
5. **Write the notes** — in plain English, the way I understood it
6. **Repeat** for the next lecture

This isn't passive learning. Every note in this repo represents something I can actually explain not just something I watched or copied.
